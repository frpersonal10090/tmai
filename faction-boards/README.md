# Base faction-board images

These are the 14 original Terra Mystica base-game faction boards used by the
in-game “Your faction board” reference panel. They were downloaded from the
individual faction pages linked by Gaming Strategy’s
[Faction Strategies](https://www.gaming-strategy.com/board-games/terra-mystica/faction-strategy/)
page on 2026-07-28. No Fire & Ice or other expansion factions are included.

The board artwork remains the property of its respective copyright holders.
